I am interested in learning about organizational innovation, particularly at the successor and precursor stages of it, in relation to resources. The topics stems from my working experience in both academia and industry.

At the initial stages of new technology emergence, I seek to understand how innovation processes unfold within organizations in terms of how resources are leveraged or created for technology creation and innovation. During my time in the biochemistry lab, I observed how researchers often rely on established processes to identify new natural compounds. Later, as a content acquisition and distribution strategist, I saw the escalation of new media technologies and formats. Together, the experience made me curious about how invention and innovation happen at early stages (e.g., How people come up with novelty in routinized settings).

This also brought up a question of what happen if new invention is exploited at scale: How does that influence the labor market or firms? Therefore, at the successor stages, I want to study what from the human capital side make a firm's success or failure in technology change and how the labor evolve along with technolgy.
